AC Repair

When the air stops being cold, the cause is almost always one of a dozen things — and guessing at it gets expensive fast. We diagnose properly, show you the failed part, and give you the repair price before we pick up a tool.

  • Blowing warm air — Low refrigerant, a failed capacitor or a compressor that isn't starting.
  • Short-cycling — Turning on and off every few minutes — usually airflow, sizing or a control fault.
  • Ice on the lines — Restricted airflow or a refrigerant charge problem. Turn it off and call us.
  • New noises — Grinding, squealing or rattling that wasn't there last summer.
  • Water where it shouldn't be — A blocked condensate drain will find your drywall before it finds the pan.

Heating Repair

North County winters are mild, which means most heating systems sit idle for months and then get asked to work on the first cold night. That's when faults show up. We service furnaces, heat pumps and hybrid systems.

  • No heat at all — Ignition, gas valve, control board or a tripped safety switch.
  • Blowing cool air — Often a limit switch or a heat-pump reversing-valve issue.
  • It runs but the house stays cold — Undersized, leaking ducts, or a system that's simply worn out.
  • A burning smell on first use — Usually harmless dust — but worth a check if it doesn't clear.
  • Clicking without ignition — A failing igniter or flame sensor. Common, and quick to fix.

Heating Installation

Indoor Air Quality

You can have a perfectly working system and still have air you don't want to breathe. Filtration, humidity and ventilation are separate problems from temperature, and they need to be treated that way.

  • High-efficiency filtration — MERV-rated media filters that actually catch what a fibreglass filter doesn't.
  • UV and air purification — In-duct treatment for microbial growth on the coil.
  • Humidity control — Coastal damp is real. Whole-home dehumidification handles it.
  • Ventilation — Fresh-air intake for tighter, newer homes that don't breathe.
  • Duct cleaning and sealing — If the ducts are dirty or leaking, nothing downstream helps.

  • Dust settles again within a day of cleaning
  • Musty smell when the system first kicks on
  • Windows fogging on the inside
  • Allergy symptoms that are worse indoors
  • Visible growth around vents or on the coil

Ductwork Services

A typical home loses a meaningful share of its conditioned air to leaking, crushed or badly-routed ducts. If one room never gets comfortable, the problem often isn't the system at all — it's what's between the system and the room.

  • Leak testing — We find where the air is going before we quote anything.
  • Sealing and repair — Mastic and proper mechanical sealing, not tape that lets go in a year.
  • Insulation — Attic ducts in Southern California need it more than most people think.
  • Redesign and rerouting — Fixing undersized returns and long, crushed runs.
  • Full replacement — When a system is being replaced and the ducts are past saving.

  • Whistling or rushing noise from the vents

Maintenance & Tune-Ups

Most of the emergency calls we take in August were preventable in May. A tune-up isn't a sales visit — it's a clean, a set of measurements, and an early warning about the part that's going to fail.

  • Two visits a year — Cooling in spring, heating in autumn.
  • Coil cleaning — Especially important within a mile or two of the ocean.
  • Electrical testing — Capacitors and contactors are the parts that fail first, and they're cheap to replace early.
  • Refrigerant and airflow check — Measured, not eyeballed.
  • Written condition report — So you know what's ageing before it strands you.
