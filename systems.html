Air quality

Temperature is only half of comfort

Filtration, humidity and ventilation are separate systems from heating and cooling, and they're where most of the "my house just feels stuffy" complaints actually live.

  • FiltrationMERV-rated media filters that catch what a supermarket fibreglass filter lets straight through.
  • HumidityWhole-home dehumidification for coastal damp, and humidification for the inland dry spells.
  • VentilationFresh-air intake for newer, tighter homes that no longer breathe on their own.
  • PurificationIn-duct UV treatment to stop microbial growth on a coil that's wet every summer.

Where to start

Nine times out of ten, in this order.

1 · Change the filterThe cheapest fix, and the one most often skipped.
2 · Check the ductsLeaks and dirt undo everything downstream of them.
3 · Clean the coilA wet, dirty evaporator coil is where the smell comes from.
4 · Then add equipmentPurifiers and dehumidifiers, once the basics are right.
